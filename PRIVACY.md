# Privacy

So Thirsty sends nothing anywhere except your own group chat (party, raid or instance), and only when a warning is set to post there. It makes no network requests.

It stores only its settings, in your WoW SavedVariables (`SoThirstyDB`) on your computer.

Other So Thirsty users in your group may exchange tiny addon messages (which version they run, whether they're a healer, and a healer's own mana percent) so only one of them posts each warning.
